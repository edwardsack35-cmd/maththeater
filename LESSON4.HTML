Lesson 4: Applications of Arithmetic Progression

Arithmetic Progressions are not only theoretical. They appear in many real-life situations. In this lesson, we will learn how to apply the nth term formula to solve practical problems.

1. Common Real-Life Applications of AP

•  Savings and money deposits
•  Salary increments
•  Taxi / transport fares
•  Seating arrangements in halls or cinemas
•  Construction (stairs, street lights, etc.)
•  Sports training plans

2. Important Formula Used

an = a + (n – 1)d

Where:
•  a = first term
•  d = common difference
•  n = number of terms / position
•  an = the required term

3. Worked Examples

Example 1: Savings

A student saves ?50 in the first week and increases the savings by ?20 every week. How much will the student save in the 8th week?

a = 50,  d = 20,  n = 8

a8 = 50 + (8 – 1) × 20
a8 = 50 + 7 × 20
a8 = 50 + 140 = ?190

Example 2: Taxi Fare

A taxi charges ?30 for the first kilometre and ?12 for every additional kilometre. What is the fare for a 10 km journey?

a = 30,  d = 12,  n = 10

a10 = 30 + (10 – 1) × 12
a10 = 30 + 9 × 12
a10 = 30 + 108 = ?138

Example 3: Seating Arrangement

In a cinema hall, the first row has 12 seats. Each next row has 3 more seats than the previous one. How many seats are in the 7th row?

a = 12,  d = 3,  n = 7

a7 = 12 + (7 – 1) × 3
a7 = 12 + 6 × 3
a7 = 12 + 18 = 30 seats

Example 4: Salary Increment

A worker earns ?800 in the first month. His salary increases by ?50 every month. What will be his salary in the 12th month?

a = 800,  d = 50,  n = 12

a12 = 800 + (12 – 1) × 50
a12 = 800 + 11 × 50
a12 = 800 + 550 = ?1,350

Example 5: Street Lights

Street lights are placed at equal distances along a road. The first light is 20 metres from the starting point and each subsequent light is 15 metres further. How far is the 9th light from the starting point?

a = 20,  d = 15,  n = 9

a9 = 20 + (9 – 1) × 15
a9 = 20 + 8 × 15
a9 = 20 + 120 = 140 metres

Exercises

Solve the following real-life problems.

Exercise 1

A student saves ?40 in the first week and increases the savings by ?15 every week. How much will he save in the 6th week?

Exercise 2

A taxi charges ?25 for the first kilometre and ?10 for every extra kilometre. What is the fare for an 8 km journey?

Exercise 3

The first row of a hall has 10 seats. Each next row has 4 more seats. How many seats are in the 5th row?

Exercise 4

A worker earns ?600 in the first month. His salary increases by ?40 every month. What will be his salary in the 10th month?

Exercise 5

The first street light is placed 25 metres from a junction and each subsequent light is 18 metres further. How far is the 7th light from the junction?

Exercise 6

A person deposits ?200 in the first month and increases the deposit by ?50 every month. How much will he deposit in the 9th month?

Remember: In real-life problems, always identify the first term (a), the common difference (d), and the required position (n) before applying the formula.
