Lesson 2: Common Difference and the nth Term of an AP

1. Common Difference (d)

In an Arithmetic Progression (AP), the difference between any two consecutive terms is always constant. This constant value is called the Common Difference and is denoted by the letter d.

d = a2 – a1  =  a3 – a2  =  a4 – a3  =…

The common difference can be:

•  Positive → the sequence is increasing
•  Negative → the sequence is decreasing
•  Zero → all terms are equal

Example 1: Find the common difference of: 5, 11, 17, 23, 29

d = 11 – 5 = 6
Check: 17 – 11 = 6,  23 – 17 = 6,  29 – 23 = 6
Therefore, d = 6

Example 2: Find the common difference of: 18, 13, 8, 3, –2

d = 13 – 18 = –5
Therefore, d = –5

Example 3: Find the common difference of: 9, 9, 9, 9, 9

d = 9 – 9 = 0
Therefore, d = 0

2. The nth Term of an Arithmetic Progression

The general term (nth term) of an Arithmetic Progression is given by the formula:

an = a + (n – 1)d

Meaning of each letter:

•  an = the term we want to find
•  a = the first term of the sequence
•  n = the position of the term (1, 2, 3, …)
•  d = the common difference

How the formula is formed

Let�s write the terms of a general AP:

1st term  =  a
2nd term  =  a + d
3rd term  =  a + 2d
4th term  =  a + 3d
…
nth term  =  a + (n – 1)d

Notice that the common difference is added (n – 1) times to reach the nth term.

3. Worked Examples

Example 1: Find the 12th term of the AP: 4, 9, 14, 19, …

a = 4,  d = 5,  n = 12

a12 = 4 + (12 – 1) × 5
a12 = 4 + 11 × 5
a12 = 4 + 55 = 59

Example 2: Find the 20th term of the AP: 10, 6, 2, –2, …

a = 10,  d = –4,  n = 20

a20 = 10 + (20 – 1) × (–4)
a20 = 10 + 19 × (–4)
a20 = 10 – 76 = –66

Example 3: The 9th term of an AP is 45 and d = 4. Find the first term.

a9 = 45,  d = 4,  n = 9

45 = a + (9 – 1) × 4
45 = a + 8 × 4
45 = a + 32
a = 45 – 32 = 13

Example 4 (with fraction):

Find the 8th term of the AP: 13, 23, 1, 43,…

a = 13,   d = 13,   n = 8

a8 = 13 + (8 – 1) × 13
a8 = 13 + 7 × 13
a8 = 13 + 73 = 83

Exercises

Practise the following questions.

1. Find the common difference of the following sequences:

(a) 7, 13, 19, 25, 31
(b) 16, 11, 6, 1, –4
(c) 4, 4, 4, 4, 4

2. Find the 12th term of the AP: 5, 9, 13, 17, …

3. Find the 15th term of the AP: 20, 14, 8, 2, …

4. The 8th term of an AP is 41 and the common difference is 4. Find the first term.

5. Find the 9th term of the AP: 12, 1, 32, 2,…

6. Which term of the AP 3, 8, 13, 18, … is 78?
