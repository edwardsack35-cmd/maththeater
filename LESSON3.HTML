Lesson 3: Finding Missing Terms in an Arithmetic Progression

In many problems, some terms of an Arithmetic Progression are missing. In this lesson, you will learn how to find those missing terms using the common difference.

1. Understanding the Problem

When terms are missing between two known terms, we first calculate the common difference and then use it to fill the blanks one by one.

Key Idea:

If there are k missing terms between two known terms, then the number of steps (gaps) between them is k + 1.

2. Formula for Common Difference when Terms are Missing

Suppose the first known term is a and the last known term is l, and there are m steps between them.

d = l – a m

Once we find d, we can find the missing terms by repeatedly adding d.

3. Step-by-Step Method

•  Step 1: Identify the first and last known terms
•  Step 2: Count the number of steps (gaps) between them
•  Step 3: Calculate the common difference using the formula above
•  Step 4: Add the common difference repeatedly to find the missing terms

4. Worked Examples

Example 1: Find the missing terms: 5, ____, ____, ____, 17

First term a = 5
Last term l = 17
Number of steps m = 4

d = 17 – 5 4 = 12 4 = 3

Now fill the missing terms:
5 + 3 = 8
8 + 3 = 11
11 + 3 = 14

Therefore, the sequence is: 5, 8, 11, 14, 17

Example 2: Find the missing terms: 20, ____, ____, 5

First term a = 20
Last term l = 5
Number of steps m = 3

d = 5 – 20 3 = –15 3 = –5

Missing terms:
20 + (–5) = 15
15 + (–5) = 10

Therefore, the sequence is: 20, 15, 10, 5

Example 3: Find the missing term: 7, ____, 19

a = 7,   l = 19,   m = 2

d = 19 – 7 2 = 12 2 = 6

Missing term = 7 + 6 = 13

Therefore, the sequence is: 7, 13, 19

Example 4: Find the missing terms: 3, ____, ____, ____, ____, 23

a = 3,   l = 23,   m = 5

d = 23 – 3 5 = 20 5 = 4

Missing terms:
3 + 4 = 7
7 + 4 = 11
11 + 4 = 15
15 + 4 = 19

Therefore, the sequence is: 3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23

Example 5 (with fraction):

Find the missing terms: 12, ____, ____, 2

a = 12,   l = 2,   m = 3

d = 2 – 12 3 = 42 – 12 3 = 32 3 = 32 × 13 = 12

Missing terms:
12 + 12 = 1
1 + 12 = 32

Therefore, the sequence is: 12, 1, 32, 2
